How to play

Rogue Bulls plant the Sour Milk Bomb at site A or B. Dairy Guard stop the plant or defuse it. Wipe the other team to win the round. First team to 8 rounds wins; sides swap at halftime.

Free-for-all is a smaller 6-cow deathmatch on dm_creamery. First to 20 kills wins. You get a short spawn shield — use it to get cover before you shoot.

Earn Gallons ($) from kills and rounds, then spend them during the buy phase. In free-for-all every kill pays out too, you can buy anywhere, and guns you buy stay with you when you respawn. Play with friends using Create private room and share the code.
