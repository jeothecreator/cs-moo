How to play

Defusal: Rogue Bulls plant the Sour Milk Bomb at site A or B; Dairy Guard stop the plant or defuse it. First team to 8 rounds wins; sides swap at halftime.

Free-for-all and 1v1 Duel: every kill earns Gallons ($). Buy anytime with B; guns you buy stay with you when you respawn.

Cow Blocks: press 6 to build. Climb your blocks and land on an enemy's head for a Moo Stomp.

Udder Chaos Obby: a solo obstacle course. Reach the golden cowbell as fast as you can.
